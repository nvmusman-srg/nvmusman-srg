# Embedded fonts

The SVGs in this folder embed subsets (printable ASCII) of these fonts as WOFF:

- **TeX Gyre Heros** (regular) and **TeX Gyre Heros Cn** (bold): GUST Font License. See `TeX-Gyre-Heros-LICENSE.txt` and `TeX-Gyre-Heros-README.txt`.
- **DejaVu Sans Mono**: Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license (free to use, modify and redistribute). See `DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`.

Brand marks in `connect.svg` (LinkedIn "in" tile, Instagram camera glyph) are simplified drawings for link identification only.
